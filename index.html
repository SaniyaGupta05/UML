About Universal Medicap

Universal Medicap Limited (UML) is a leading manufacturer of primary pharmaceutical packaging components and value-added services, delivering quality and compliance worldwide.

Universal Medicap Limited combines technical expertise, advanced manufacturing, and stringent quality systems to deliver reliable pharmaceutical packaging components worldwide. Our mission is built on innovation, compliance, and customer-centric solutions.

Driven by innovation, quality, and customer focus, Universal Medicap Limited is committed to building long-term partnerships with pharmaceutical manufacturers. Through continuous improvement, responsible manufacturing practices, and technical expertise, UML strives to support global healthcare needs with dependable, compliant, and sustainable packaging solutions.
